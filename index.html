01 / THE PREMISE

Expertise gets us through the door.
Uncertainty makes it interesting.

Long-form conversations with people living at the edge of what we know.

Lexx follows each guest’s journey into the questions that still remain. The aim is not only to understand their work, but to zoom out: how it changed them, how it feels from the inside, and what they see when they look back at themselves.
